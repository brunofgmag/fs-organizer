Introduction

Faro Airport serves the Algarve coast of southern Portugal. This scenery models the whole airport with its terminal, apron, cargo area and the lagoon to the south.

Installation

Keep the folder in your Community folder and disable any other scenery of LPFR, including the one that comes with the simulator, so the two do not overlap.

Features

Ground polygons from recent imagery, custom terminal interiors, animated jetways and night lighting tuned for approaches over the water.

Performance

Textures are streamed in three levels of detail. On systems with 8 GB of video memory, choose the medium texture preset in the scenery configurator.
