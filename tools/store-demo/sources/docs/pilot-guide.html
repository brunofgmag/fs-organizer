C172 Classic Pilot Guide

About the aircraft

A four-seat, high-wing trainer with a carburetted piston engine and steam gauges.

The Classic model reproduces a well-used club aircraft, dents and all.

ItemValue
Engine160 hp, four cylinders
Fuel40 US gal usable
Cruise110 kt TAS

Before flight

Walk around the aircraft on the EFB and drain the fuel sumps.

Check the oil level and the tyre pressure before every flight.

Starting the engine

Mixture rich, carburettor heat cold, prime three strokes in cold weather.

Throttle open a quarter inch, clear the propeller, master on, key to START.

Takeoff and climb

Flaps up for a normal takeoff. Rotate at 55 kt and climb at 74 kt.

Landing
